# My Top Favorite K-Dramas

---

## 1. Bloodhounds
> *"When the law fails to protect the weak, fists step in."*

An intense action-thriller following two young boxers who team up with a benevolent lender to bring down a ruthless loan shark.

* **Genre:** Action, Crime, Thriller

---

## 2. Weak Hero Class 1
> *"I told you to stop."*

A studious top student uses his intelligence, fast reflexes, and psychology to fight back against the violent bullies inside and outside his high school.

* **Genre:** Action, Youth, Thriller

---

## 3. My Name
> *"I will become a monster to kill the one who killed my father."*

Driven by revenge following her father's murder, a woman joins an underground crime syndicate and infiltrates the police force as a mole.

* **Genre:** Action, Crime, Noir, Thriller

---

## 4. Mercy for None
> *"Severing ties with the underworld is never easy."*

A gritty noir story following a former gangster who re-enters the dark underworld after his younger brother's sudden death to uncover the truth.

* **Genre:** Action, Crime, Drama

---

## 5. A Killer Paradox
> *"Is killing a killer a crime, or divine justice?"*

An ordinary college student accidentally kills a serial killer, realizing he possesses a supernatural ability to sense evil—triggering a relentless cat-and-mouse chase with a sharp detective.

* **Genre:** Dark Comedy, Crime, Thriller, Mystery

---

## 6. Our Beloved Summer
> *"We broke up because we hated each other, but the internet brought us back together."*

A heartwarming coming-of-age romantic comedy about two ex-lovers whose high school documentary goes viral ten years later, forcing them back into each other's lives.

* **Genre:** Romance, Comedy, Coming-of-age

---

## 7. Love Next Door
> *"Reconnecting with the person who knows all your embarrassing childhood secrets."*

A woman trying to reset her chaotic life returns to her hometown and reconnects with her mom's friend's son—a successful architect who shared her youth.

* **Genre:** Romantic Comedy, Slice of Life

---

## 8. King the Land
> *"A smile forced by work meets a smile born from genuine joy."*

An heir to a luxury hotel conglomerate who despises fake smiles collides with a hardworking hotelier famous for her irresistible smile.

* **Genre:** Romance, Comedy

---

## 9. Fight for My Way
> *"Chasing big dreams despite having 'underqualified' resumes."*

Four long-time friends navigate life's struggles and pursue their unconventional dreams despite lacking traditional credentials, blossoming into unexpected romance along the way.

* **Genre:** Romance, Comedy, Slice of Life, Sports

---

## 10. Gyeongseong Creature
> *"In the darkest era of 1945, survival meant fighting both humans and monsters."*

A historical horror-thriller set in 1945 Gyeongseong (Seoul), where a wealthy pawnshop owner and a tracker fight for survival against a creature born from secret human experiments.

* **Genre:** Historical, Horror, Sci-Fi, Romance
